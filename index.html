Hi there, I'm Marco López!

I am an NSF MPS-Ascend Postdoctoral Fellow in Mathematics at the City University of New York. Read more about me...

Research

My research is in dynamical systems. Specifically, thermodynamics formalism related to iterated function systems and symbolic dynamical systems. That includes the metric and dimension theory of shrinking target sets, and computability theory of some dynamical quantities. Check out my publications page.

Other projects

That said, my mathematical interests are much broader. In particular, I'm always interested in learning about mathematical applications to other disciplines. For example, upon learning about the lives and works of Louis Bachelier and Jim Simons I became interested in mathematical finance, which compelled me to work on some small exploratory projects.
